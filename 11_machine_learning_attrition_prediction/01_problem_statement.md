📌 Business Problem Restatement

Employee attrition results in financial loss, reduced productivity, and loss of institutional knowledge.
While historical analysis identifies why employees left, the organization now requires a predictive system
to identify employees who are at risk of leaving.

🎯 Machine Learning Goal

To build a classification model that:

Predicts the probability of employee attrition

Identifies high-risk employees early

Supports proactive HR intervention strategies

🧠 ML Problem Type

Type: Binary Classification

Target Variable: Attrition

Yes → 1

No → 0

📊 Success Criteria

High Recall for attrition cases

Balanced Precision–Recall tradeoff

Model interpretability for HR stakeholders
