📊 Employee Attrition Analysis | End-to-End HR Analytics Project

I’m excited to share my latest HR Analytics project focused on Employee Attrition Analysis, built using the IBM HR Analytics Dataset.

Employee attrition is not just an HR metric — it directly impacts cost, productivity, and organizational stability.
This project demonstrates how data can be transformed into actionable business insights and lays a strong foundation for predictive modeling using Machine Learning.

🔍 Problem Statement

High employee attrition leads to increased recruitment costs, loss of skilled talent, and operational disruptions.
The goal of this project was to identify the key drivers of attrition and provide data-backed HR recommendations.

🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)

SQL (data validation & insights)

Jupyter Notebook

Data Visualization & Storytelling

Business & HR Analytics

📈 Key Insights

Employees working overtime show significantly higher attrition

Lower compensation strongly correlates with employee exits

Sales and HR departments experience higher attrition rates

Early-career employees are more vulnerable to attrition

Certain job roles (Sales Executives, Lab Technicians) are high-risk segments

💡 Business Impact

Highlights critical workforce risk areas

Enables HR teams to take proactive retention actions

Supports data-driven decisions on workload, compensation, and engagement

Prepares the organization for predictive attrition modeling
